About

I sit between delivery and engineering. At Tesco I run programmes that cut across engineering, product, analytics and creative teams, and I still write code most weeks, partly because I like knowing how the thing I'm coordinating actually works.

I graduated from the University of Portsmouth in 2026 with a BSc (Hons) in Software Engineering, and became a Certified Scrum Product Owner the same year. Before any of that I was a junior software engineer at a garden-tech startup, writing PHP and JavaScript.

Day to day that means JavaScript, Python (just a bit), PHP and Laravel, Java, SQL, and a great deal of Jira, Confluence and Excel. I've load-tested things on Azure, built a footfall detector with YOLO, and can still hand-write HTML and CSS, which is how this page exists.

Outside work I coach American football with the London Blitz B Team (BAFCA Level 1 in progress), volunteer with the Kent Digital Champions Network, and am part of the Sea Cadet Corps. I also play chess, not well, which is why there's a board further down.
